    About the Kitchen

    घर का स्वाद, अपनों जैसी देखभाल

    Charroti started as one home kitchen cooking for a handful of neighbours who missed proper ghar ka khana. It's grown, but the way we cook hasn't — every dal is still tempered to order, every roti still comes off the tawa a few minutes before it reaches you.

    We buy what's fresh at the local mandi each morning, cook in small batches, and keep the kitchen the way we'd want our own family's food handled — clean, unhurried, and seasoned with a little more care than a menu can really describe.
